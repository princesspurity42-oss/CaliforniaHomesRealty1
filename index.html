The office

A small office with a long memory of this coastline.

California Homes Realty is an independent real estate agency at 4401 Capitola Road, Suite 4 — a short walk from the village, the wharf and the beaches that make this stretch of the Monterey Bay so sought after.

We are not a call centre and we are not a franchise. Clients work directly with the people who live here, know which streets catch the afternoon fog, which blocks sit above the flood line, and what a property is actually worth before it ever hits a listing site.

That is why our reviews tend to use the same two words: they listen. Whether you are a first-time buyer stretching for a condo in Live Oak or a family selling a home you have held for thirty years, the process starts with understanding what you actually need — not with a sales pitch.

Market snapshot

What the Capitola market looks like right now.

Coastal Santa Cruz County is a small, supply-constrained market where a handful of sales can move the median. Treat any single figure as a starting point, not a valuation.

$1.23M Typical home value in Capitola, September 2026 (Zillow), up 7.5% year over year.
$1.82M Median sale price for single-family homes, September 2026 (Property in Santa Cruz).
$766K Median sale price for condominiums and townhomes, September 2026 (Property in Santa Cruz).

Figures are drawn from published third-party market reports for September 2026 and are reproduced here for context only. Different sources measure differently — Redfin, for example, reported a $1.4M median for the three months ending September 2026. For a valuation of a specific property, speak with us directly.
